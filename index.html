Weekly 8-Point Physical Checkup

Perform this quick exam weekly to catch early signs of illness or discomfort.

1. Incisors & Dry Chin

Check that top and bottom teeth meet evenly without overgrowth. Ensure the chin is dry—drooling indicates dental malocclusion or pain.

2. Silent Breathing

Hold your piggy near your ear. Breathing should be completely silent. Clicking, rattling, or wheezing indicates upper respiratory infections (URI).

3. Clear Eyes & Nose

Eyes should be bright and clear with no cloudiness. Nose must be free of crust, discharge, or sneezing.

4. Plentiful Droppings

Poops should be uniform, firm, and oval-shaped. Sudden changes in size or consistency mean gut stasis or dietary upset.

5. Clean Footpads

Check bottoms of feet for redness, swelling, or sores (bumblefoot). Ensure bedding stays dry and clean.

6. Coat & Skin Check

Part the fur to check for dandruff, scabs, bald patches, or microscopic parasites like mites or lice.

7. Dry Bottom & Urine

The rear end should be clean and dry. Watch for urine scalding or chalky white sludge buildup.

8. Grease Gland Maintenance

Located just above the tail base. Boars especially can build up waxy oils that require gentle cleaning with coconut oil or mild soap.

🚫 FATAL ANTIBIOTIC WARNING

Penicillin, Amoxicillin, Clavamox, Ampicillin, and Erythromycin wipe out good gut bacteria and are fatal to guinea pigs. Safe options prescribed by exotic vets include Baytril (Enrofloxacin) and Bactrim.
