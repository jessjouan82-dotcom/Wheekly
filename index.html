Weekly 8-Point Physical Examination Guide

Perform this structured physical audit weekly. Expand each section for clinical assessment criteria.

1. Incisors & Dry Chin

Evaluation: Front incisors should meet evenly without overlapping or spurs. Chin must be dry.

Action: Drooling or wet chin indicates molar malocclusion or pain. Schedule veterinary exam.

2. Silent Respiration

Evaluation: Breathing should be effortless and completely silent.

Action: Clicking or wheezing indicates Upper Respiratory Infection (URI) requiring immediate antibiotics.

3. Ocular & Nasal Clarity

Evaluation: Eyes clear, bright, free of cloudiness. Nostrils clean and dry.

Action: Milky discharge or nasal crusting signals infection or environmental allergens.

4. Gastrointestinal Output

Evaluation: High volume of firm, uniform, oval pellets.

Action: Small or clustered poops indicate decreased fiber intake or onset of stasis.

5. Footpad Integrity

Evaluation: Smooth skin without redness, swelling, or scabs (pododermatitis / bumblefoot).

Action: Improve bedding hygiene and consult a vet if open sores develop.

6. Coat & Skin Audit

Evaluation: Part fur to inspect skin for flakes, baldness, or excessive scratching.

Action: Flaking or V-shaped hair loss indicates burrowing mites or fungal infection.

7. Perineal Hygiene

Evaluation: Rear end dry and clean. Audit boars for sac impaction.

Action: Wetness indicates urinary issues or impaction in aging males.

8. Grease Gland Assessment

Evaluation: Located on the rump. Check for waxy secretion buildup.

Action: Clean periodically with mild pet shampoo or coconut oil to prevent dermatitis.

Privacy notice

What we store. Your guinea pig profiles, weights, health, feeding and treatment logs. By default this stays in your browser's local storage on this device only. Clearing your browser data deletes it, so use Export to keep a backup.

If you sign in. Your email address and the same data are stored with our hosting provider (Supabase) so they can sync across your devices. Only you can read your own data. Signing in is optional.

Analytics. We use Vercel Analytics to count page visits. Fonts, icons and scripts are loaded from third-party CDNs, which means those providers can see your IP address when the page loads.

Your choices. You can export your data at any time. To delete your cloud data and account, email the site owner. Deleting browser data removes the local copy.
